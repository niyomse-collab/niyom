Niyomsil Design AI Enhancer is a separate application for NIYOMSIL DESIGN / นิยมศิลป์ดีไซน์.

This repository and its builds do not push to or modify the upstream ARM AI Image Enhancer repository.

The V2 ARM Core build uses locally included processing source corresponding to the tested ARM V2.2.8 component set:
- DeviceManager
- EngineManager
- RealESRGANEngine

Real-ESRGAN, BasicSR, PyTorch, TorchVision and the RealESRGAN_x4plus model remain subject to their respective upstream licenses and terms.

The Niyomsil Design logo and branding are user-provided assets.
